PARTICIPANT INFORMATION

EVENT RULES.

Please read these important rules before registering. These are a concise recreation of the publicly available event rules; refer to the organiser's official rules for the latest authoritative version.

Age eligibility

  • 5K: minimum age 5 years.
  • 10K and 21K: minimum age 15 years.
  • Age is calculated on event day. Proof of age may be requested.

Registration and BIB

  • Individual entries only; registrations are non-transferable.
  • Customised BIB requests require registration at least four days before the event.
  • BIBs are collected at the announced expo before race day; no event-day distribution.
  • No on-the-spot registration.
  • Display your BIB clearly on the front of your chest.

Safety and conduct

  • Participants must be medically fit and run at their own risk.
  • Follow instructions from event officials, police and medical staff.
  • No pets, unregistered runners or unauthorised participation on the course.
  • Maintain sportsmanship and do not litter.
  • Personal insurance is the participant's responsibility.

Cancellation and changes

  • Registrations are generally non-refundable and non-cancellable.
  • In the event of cancellation or postponement, the organiser's published force majeure policy applies.
  • Organisers may modify event details, route, timings and rules when required.

Declaration

By registering, participants confirm their details are accurate, agree to follow event instructions, acknowledge the risks of participation and consent to event photography and media use as set out in the official terms.
